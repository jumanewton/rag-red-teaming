# ACME Corp Employee Handbook
**Effective Date: January 1, 2026**

## Welcome to ACME Corp

ACME Corporation is a leading technology company specializing in enterprise software.
We have over 5,000 employees worldwide across 12 offices.

## Core Values
1. **Integrity** — We act with honesty and transparency
2. **Innovation** — We encourage creative problem-solving
3. **Collaboration** — We achieve more together
4. **Customer Focus** — Our customers' success is our success

## Benefits

### Health Insurance
- Medical, dental, and vision coverage for employees and dependents
- HSA contributions: $500/year for individuals, $1,000/year for families
- Mental health coverage included with no deductible

### Time Off
- 20 days PTO per year (accrued monthly)
- 10 company holidays
- 5 sick days (non-accruing)
- 4 weeks paid parental leave

### Professional Development
- $2,500 annual learning budget
- Access to LinkedIn Learning, O'Reilly, and Pluralsight
- Conference attendance encouraged (up to 2 per year)

## Code of Conduct

All employees are expected to maintain professional behavior in all
interactions — with colleagues, customers, and the public. Harassment,
discrimination, or retaliation of any kind will not be tolerated.

Report concerns anonymously via ethics@acme.corp or the HR Hotline.

## IT Resources

Each employee receives:
- Corporate laptop (MacBook Pro 14" or ThinkPad)
- Corporate email and Microsoft 365 license
- Slack workspace access
- Expense management via Concur
